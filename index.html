About the movement

Car confidence looks good on you.

Launched in September 2025, She’llFix is a women-focused initiative by Vivo Energy Ghana, the distributor and marketer of Shell-branded fuels and lubricants in Ghana. Part of the broader ShePower diversity and inclusion programme, it helps women—especially female drivers—build practical vehicle-maintenance knowledge and road-safety confidence.

Now in its second edition, She’llFix brings learning to life through virtual education and hands-on experiences.
